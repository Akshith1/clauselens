# Policy form sources

## Committed forms

| File | Form | Source |
|---|---|---|
| CL-HO-01.txt | Sample Homeowners Policy - Special Form | Synthetic, written for this project |
| CL-HO-E1.txt | Water Backup and Sump Discharge Coverage Endorsement | Synthetic, written for this project |

The committed forms are synthetic so the repo can be public and tests stay deterministic.
They follow the usual conventions of homeowners forms (outline numbering, quoted defined
terms, "This endorsement modifies…" blocks) without copying any real form.

## Real forms (kept out of git)

Specimen forms from state insurance department filings are public records, but many are
copyrighted by their authors (for example, rating bureaus). Put them in `data/forms/private/`
(git-ignored), add them to `data/forms/private/catalog.json` in the same format as
`catalog.json`, and record them below for your own reference.
