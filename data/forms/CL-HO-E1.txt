WATER BACKUP AND SUMP DISCHARGE COVERAGE ENDORSEMENT
This is a synthetic sample endorsement written for the ClauseLens project. It is not a real insurance endorsement.
THIS ENDORSEMENT CHANGES THE POLICY. PLEASE READ IT CAREFULLY.
This endorsement modifies insurance provided under the following:
CL-HO-01 SAMPLE HOMEOWNERS POLICY - SPECIAL FORM

SCHEDULE
A. Limit Of Insurance for this endorsement: $10,000 per "occurrence".

ENDORSEMENT PROVISIONS
A. Coverage
1. Paragraph A.2.b. in Section I - Exclusions is deleted.
2. The following is added to Section I - Perils Insured Against, Paragraph B.:
a. Water or waterborne material which backs up through sewers or drains or which overflows or is discharged from a sump, sump pump or related equipment, even if the overflow or discharge results from mechanical breakdown.
B. Limit
1. The most we will pay for loss covered by this endorsement is the limit shown in the Schedule, regardless of the number of locations or the number of "occurrences" during a single day.
C. Deductible
1. A deductible of $500 applies to each loss covered by this endorsement. No other deductible applies to the same loss.
D. Exclusion
1. We do not cover loss caused by your negligence in failing to maintain a sump pump in working condition.
